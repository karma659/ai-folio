My work sits at the intersection of quantitative finance and software engineering. I enjoy taking an open question, exploring the data and building a system that makes the answer useful.

At AIMPaaS, I developed infrastructure for portfolio operations. At FBN Securities, I built tools for institutional research. My work at WorldQuant focused on developing and backtesting alpha signals across global equities.

Earlier, I worked on automation and AI tools at Goprac. I hold an MS in Finance with a FinTech concentration from Baruch College and a Bachelor of Engineering from Jadavpur University.
